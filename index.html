PDF Pocket – Scan & Compress: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

PDF Pocket (“the app”) is an Android app for scanning documents, creating, merging, editing and compressing PDF files, and converting between PDF and Word. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

In short

Your documents never leave your phone. Everything the app does with your files is processed on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your files. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which is described below.

Your files stay on your device

All the work the app does — scanning, cropping, text recognition (OCR), merging, editing, compression and conversion — runs locally on your phone. Your documents, photos and the files the app creates are never sent to the developer or to any third party. Files you create are stored in the app’s private storage on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. You can share or save them elsewhere only when you choose to use “Share” or “Save to…”.

Permissions

The app uses the Internet permission only to show ads. It requests no other Android permissions. When you scan a document, the camera opens through your phone’s own camera app, and you choose which files and photos to open through the system file picker. The app can only see the files you explicitly select.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location or documents, and the app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and full-screen ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Your documents and files are never shared with AdMob. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice later from “Ad privacy settings” in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Third-party components

The app uses open-source libraries (such as PdfBox-Android and OpenCV) and Google ML Kit text recognition. The text-recognition models are bundled inside the app and run on-device; they do not send your content over the network. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, as described above.

Password-protected PDFs

If you open a password-protected PDF, the password you type is used only in memory to open that file and is not stored or transmitted.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

We do not store your files or personal data on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the app holds on your phone, delete the files from within the app or uninstall it. Data held by Google for advertising is managed through your Google account and Android ad settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date will change.
